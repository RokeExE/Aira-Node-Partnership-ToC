# Aira Node Partnership Terms

> [!IMPORTANT]
> By accepting a partnership with **Aira Node**, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms.

Thank you for your interest in partnering with **Aira Node**.

Our partnership program is designed to support promising projects while maintaining a fair, reliable, and secure hosting environment for everyone.

---

## Partnership Benefits

| Project Type                   | Potential Benefit                                         |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Completely free projects       | Free hosting, subject to approval and available resources |
| Projects with premium features | Discounts on Aira Node services                           |
| Paid commands or subscriptions | Benefits based on pricing and partnership level           |
| Growing projects               | Partnership benefits may be adjusted as the project grows |

> [!NOTE]
> Partnership benefits are reviewed **case-by-case** and may be adjusted when necessary.

---

## Access Requirements

To provide proper support and maintain service quality, Aira Node may require reasonable access to information related to the hosted project.

Aira Node may:

* Review relevant logs when necessary
* Review resource usage
* Access relevant service information for troubleshooting
* Review information for security purposes
* Review information when required for compliance

> [!WARNING]
> Refusing a required access request may result in the partnership being **suspended or terminated**.

---

## Activity Requirements

Partnered projects are expected to remain active and maintained.

Partners should:

* Keep their project reasonably active
* Maintain their hosted services
* Notify Aira Node before planned extended downtime

### Inactivity Policy

If a partnered bot, application, website, game server, or other hosted service remains inactive for **more than 14 consecutive days** without prior notice, the partnership may be terminated.

If you plan to temporarily take your project offline, notify Aira Node in advance.

---

## Resource Usage

Partnership hosting is provided to help projects grow and is **not unlimited infrastructure**.

Partners must use resources fairly and responsibly.

This includes:

* CPU
* RAM
* Storage
* Bandwidth
* Other infrastructure resources

Excessive resource usage that negatively impacts Aira Node infrastructure may result in:

**Limitation → Suspension → Termination**

Aira Node may also adjust resource allocations when necessary to maintain overall infrastructure stability.

---

## Security

Partners are responsible for keeping their hosted services secure.

Hosted projects should:

* Keep software properly maintained
* Apply important security updates
* Avoid known vulnerabilities
* Follow applicable platform requirements

Aira Node reserves the right to suspend services that pose a security risk to:

* Aira Node infrastructure
* Aira Node users
* Other hosted projects

---

## Backups

> [!WARNING]
> Partners are responsible for maintaining their own backups unless otherwise stated.

Aira Node will make reasonable efforts to maintain service reliability, but **cannot guarantee recovery of lost data**.

Partners are strongly encouraged to maintain independent backups of important files and databases.

---

## Fair Use

The partnership program is intended for **genuine projects**.

Partnership resources may not be:

* Resold
* Shared with third parties
* Leased
* Transferred

unless explicitly approved by Aira Node.

> [!CAUTION]
> Abuse of partnership benefits may result in **immediate termination**.
